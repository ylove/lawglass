# Locate evaluation note (#295)

The automated suite uses synthetic source text and injected providers and fetchers; it makes no network calls. It checks source substring verification (including whitespace and punctuation normalization), original span boundaries, all five labels, repeated retained/removed wording, fabricated quotes, forged prompt wrappers, citation filtering, conditions in receipts, budgets, version overlap/caps, and the UI's not-found wording. A pipeline test retrieves a synthetic grants clause and verifies the selected source words and citation end to end.

These checks establish the deterministic guarantees. They do not measure how often a real selection model finds the right section for a real news sentence. No claim of 24/30 retrieval accuracy is made.

The issue's separate field evaluation remains to be run: choose about ten public claims for each of three real bills, drawn from sponsor releases, official summaries, or public posts. A human should record the sentence, source URL, loaded version, expected section(s), and whether the relevant wording is retained, inserted, or removed before running locate. Record top-section accuracy (target: at least 24 of 30), struck text labelled current (target: zero), and non-source quotes (target: zero). Include in the claim set (a) sentences that quote an amended provision, where the live wording sits next to struck wording (for example a fee changed from one amount to another), and (b) claims whose wording appears twice in one section, once struck and once retained. Both were failure modes found in review of the first version; the expected result is the retained or edited occurrence with a truthful label, never the struck copy shown as current. Keep downloaded legislation in the ignored fixtures directory; publish only claim metadata and aggregate results. Use the actual answer provider, model, and reranker intended for the release and record their versions.

Earlier-version overlap is lexical, not semantic. It reports the fraction of distinct content words in the best local window; it cannot establish that a version makes the claim, and its UI intentionally supplies no verdict. The frozen response contract carries a version URL and excerpt but no earlier-version section locator.
